Maria's Fountain Pen Collection

TWSBI Eco Transparent Yellow

This was the first fancy-ish pen that I bought, and the one that drew me into the hobby. It has occasional flow issues towards the end of a writing (or drawing) session, but the nib feels amazingly smooth against paper. I've only ever used the ink Vert Olive by Jacques Herbin in it (guess I got used to it).

TWSBI Eco Glow Purple

My first pen with a stub nib! In the dark it glows a soothing faint blue color. Definitely worth the purchase, though I ran into trouble when I first filled it. The pen can be finicky with dry inks, and I had chosen the both dry and shimmery ink Cat's Eye Nebula by Colorverse (without any lubricant added).

Pilot Kakuno Transparent Blue

My first Pilot Kakuno! It's part of the family series so it has a cute smiley face on the nib. I selected the fine nib, and was suprised and impressed by the writing feel and smooth ink flow (especially considering its price)! It's super beginner friendly and has performed well with most inks I have tried.

Pilot Kakuno Transparent Pink

Loved the blue Kakuno so much that I got another pen from the family series in pink with a medium nib. I love how nostalgic the transparent colors of this series are! The medium nib remains a fine line (as Japanese medium nibs usually do), but has just enough thickness to show off ink qualities.
